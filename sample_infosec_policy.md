# Prairie Mutual Insurance — Information Security Policy

**Version:** 2.1  **Effective:** January 1, 2026  **Owner:** Chief Information Security Officer
*Fictional company. Created as sample data for a policy gap assessment.*

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes requirements for protecting Prairie Mutual's information systems and data. It applies to all employees, contractors, and systems owned or operated by Prairie Mutual.

## 2. Policy Governance

2.1 The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) owns this policy and is accountable for the company's information security program.

2.2 This policy is reviewed and approved by the CISO and the Chief Operating Officer at least annually, and after any significant change to the business or regulatory environment.

2.3 The policy is published on the company intranet. Employees acknowledge it upon hire.

2.4 Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

3.1 **CISO:** sets security strategy, approves exceptions, and reports on security to the executive team.

3.2 **IT Department:** implements and operates technical controls described in this policy.

3.3 **Managers:** ensure their staff follow this policy and approve access requests for their teams.

3.4 **All personnel:** protect company information and report suspected security incidents.

## 4. Risk Assessment

4.1 The Information Security team performs a formal risk assessment of critical systems annually. The assessment identifies threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, and impact.

4.2 Risks rated High must have a documented treatment plan approved by the CISO within 60 days.

## 5. Access Control

5.1 Access is granted based on job responsibilities and must be approved by the user's manager.

5.2 Passwords must be at least 8 characters.

5.3 Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for remote VPN access.

5.4 Access is removed when an employee leaves the company.

5.5 Administrator privileges are granted only as appropriate.

## 6. Data Protection

6.1 Confidential data, including policyholder personal information, must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher.

6.2 Confidential data may not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage.

6.3 Data is retained according to the company records retention schedule and securely destroyed afterward.

## 7. System Security and Patching

7.1 All servers and workstations must use the company's approved secure configuration baseline.

7.2 Critical security patches must be applied within 14 days of release; all other patches within 30 days.

7.3 Unsupported (end-of-life) software must be removed or approved as an exception by the CISO.

## 8. Backups

8.1 Critical systems and data are backed up nightly.

8.2 Backups are stored at a secondary location.

## 9. Incident Response

9.1 Employees must report suspected security incidents to the IT Help Desk immediately.

9.2 IT will investigate reported incidents and take appropriate action.

## 10. Exceptions

10.1 Exceptions to this policy must be requested in writing and approved by the CISO. Approved exceptions are reviewed annually.
