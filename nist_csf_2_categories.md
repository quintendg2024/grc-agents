# NIST CSF 2.0 — Category Reference

Summary of the 22 categories in the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0, grouped by its six Functions. Descriptions are short paraphrases for use as an assessment checklist. Official framework: https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework

## GOVERN (GV) — Establish and monitor the organization's cybersecurity strategy, expectations, and policy

| ID | Category | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| GV.OC | Organizational Context | Understanding the mission, stakeholders, and legal/regulatory requirements that shape cybersecurity decisions |
| GV.RM | Risk Management Strategy | Defined risk appetite, tolerance, and how cybersecurity risk decisions are made |
| GV.RR | Roles, Responsibilities, and Authorities | Who is accountable for cybersecurity, with assigned roles and leadership oversight |
| GV.PO | Policy | Cybersecurity policy is established, communicated, enforced, and reviewed |
| GV.OV | Oversight | Results of risk management activities are reviewed and used to adjust strategy |
| GV.SC | Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management | Managing risk from vendors, suppliers, and third-party service providers |

## IDENTIFY (ID) — Understand current cybersecurity risks

| ID | Category | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| ID.AM | Asset Management | Inventory of hardware, software, data, and services, managed by criticality |
| ID.RA | Risk Assessment | Identifying threats, vulnerabilities, and their likelihood and impact |
| ID.IM | Improvement | Lessons learned from assessments, tests, and incidents drive improvements |

## PROTECT (PR) — Use safeguards to manage risks

| ID | Category | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| PR.AA | Identity Management, Authentication, and Access Control | Managing identities, authentication (including MFA), and least-privilege access |
| PR.AT | Awareness and Training | Security awareness and role-based training for personnel |
| PR.DS | Data Security | Protecting confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data at rest and in transit; backups |
| PR.PS | Platform Security | Secure configuration, patching, and maintenance of hardware and software |
| PR.IR | Technology Infrastructure Resilience | Network protection, segmentation, and capacity/resilience of infrastructure |

## DETECT (DE) — Find and analyze possible attacks and compromises

| ID | Category | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| DE.CM | Continuous Monitoring | Monitoring networks, systems, and activity (logging) for potential threats |
| DE.AE | Adverse Event Analysis | Analyzing anomalies and events to identify and characterize incidents |

## RESPOND (RS) — Take action on a detected incident

| ID | Category | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| RS.MA | Incident Management | An incident response plan is executed; incidents are triaged and escalated |
| RS.AN | Incident Analysis | Investigating incidents to understand scope and root cause |
| RS.CO | Incident Response Reporting and Communication | Notifying internal and external stakeholders, including regulators, as required |
| RS.MI | Incident Mitigation | Containing and eradicating incidents |

## RECOVER (RC) — Restore assets and operations affected by an incident

| ID | Category | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| RC.RP | Incident Recovery Plan Execution | Restoring systems and data, including verifying backups and recovery |
| RC.CO | Incident Recovery Communication | Coordinating and communicating recovery activities with stakeholders |
